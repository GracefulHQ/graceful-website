Privacy policy for text messages

Last updated November 1, 2026

Who we are

The Graceful Company, LLC. Contact: hi@thegracefulcompany.com, (954) 593-1222.

What we collect for text messages

Your mobile number, your agreement to receive texts and when you gave it, and the texts and photos we send and receive.

How we use it

Only to send the kinds of texts listed on our texts page (https://www.thegracefulcompany.com/texts/) and to answer you.

Sharing

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.

We share it only with the providers that deliver our texts, and only to deliver them.

How long we keep it

As long as you receive our texts, and as the law requires after that. To have your number removed, email hi@thegracefulcompany.com.

Stopping texts

Reply STOP to any text. Reply HELP for help.
